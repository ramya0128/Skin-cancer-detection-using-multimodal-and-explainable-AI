## Unresolved project-note citations

- **“O-Shap”:** an exact-name arXiv query returned no result. A broad metadata search found the related text-tabular SHAP preprint above, but did not establish that it is O-Shap. Keep the name uncited until the intended title/DOI is supplied or independently verified.
- **“PRISM”:** the combined exact-term arXiv search did not identify a relevant multimodal explainability paper. The returned record was unrelated quantum physics. The name is ambiguous; do not cite or make a novelty distinction from it without an exact reference.
- **“Jin et al., AAAI 2022” and “Jin et al., MedIA 2023”:** not uniquely resolved from author/year/venue in this focused search. Crossref verifies a different 2023 Jin et al. article in *MethodsX* (above); it is not automatically the cited MedIA work. Exact titles or DOIs are needed before describing H-MSFI or comparing those methods.

## Novelty assessment

1. **Do not claim** a new Shapley algorithm, the first multimodal explanation framework, the first synthetic explanation benchmark, or the first multimodal skin-lesion model. The verified work above predates or directly overlaps those broad formulations.
2. The most defensible *candidate* contribution is narrower: a reproducible evaluation of explanations for patient-level image-plus-clinical late fusion, separating (a) a specified exact two-player CWF Shapley game, (b) branch-specific feature/pixel explanations, and (c) controlled planted cues with known image/tabular reliability and interactions. Attribution-versus-intervention agreement could be a useful empirical question, not a guaranteed contribution.
3. This candidate is **not yet established**. Phase F faithfulness/stability is blocked under the read-only decision; Phase G cue experiments are not run; LEMON, GridVQA-X, the 2026 cross-modal synergy benchmark, and the same-dataset Mixed Fusion paper require full-protocol comparison. There are no lesion boxes or concept masks in the current saved artifacts, so H-MSFI-style claims are not currently supported.
4. Report exact grouped TreeSHAP as exact only for its eight-player game, background, and selected payoff. Report modality Shapley as exact only for its specified two-player game. Neither constitutes an unconditional ground truth about disease or patient-level causal relevance.

## Read-only search scope and limitations

Sources checked included arXiv records for LEMON, cross-modal synergy, PAD-UFES-20 papers, SHAP, saliency sanity checks, CARLA, and Hooker et al.; Crossref DOI metadata for GridVQA-X, CLEVR-XAI, Jin et al. (MethodsX), PAD-UFES-20, Kawahara et al., and the 2026 same-dataset classifier; and the Research Square preprint page for the text-tabular SHAP work. No dataset was downloaded, no model was run, and no test data were accessed.

This focused pass establishes several close overlaps and unresolved citations, not exhaustive novelty. In particular, full-text comparisons remain necessary for GridVQA-X, LEMON, the MethodsX paper, and the same-dataset 2026 works before final claims are written.
